PokéSurvivor moves and their evolutions

There are 118 moves across all 18 types. Your Pokémon attacks on its own with the active one, and when you level up you pick cards to learn new moves or upgrade them up to level 5. 107 of them can evolve: max them out, get the upgrade card they ask for (Power, Vigor, Reach, Focus…) and a golden evolution card will appear. Burst moves (Hyper Beam, Explosion, Protect, Recover, Thunder Wave, Draco Meteor) work differently: they have a long cooldown, charge while you use your other moves and fire the moment you switch to them. They can't be bonded, and you can carry two.
